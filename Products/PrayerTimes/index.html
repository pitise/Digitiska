    Azan

    Azan and alerts only work while this app is open on your device. A web app cannot wake a closed or locked phone.

    Install

    iPhone / iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: browser menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

    Privacy

    No account, no ads, no analytics. Your position stays on this device. To show a place name, a position rounded to about 1 km is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim.

    Times are calculated on this device and can differ by a minute or two from the official e-solat timetable.
